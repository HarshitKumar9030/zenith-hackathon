# Extra pickup weight

Weight above the included per-pickup allowance costs **₹3/kg**, based on helper-recorded net weight. Fractions are proportional, rounded to the nearest paise: 0.5 kg extra costs ₹1.50. Basic and reward pickups include 2 kg, Gold 8 kg and Platinum 10 kg. Gold/Platinum continue to include segregation; other sorting and evening fees remain separate.

The booking flow discloses the rate before collection. The measured receipt itemises excess kilograms and the extra charge; resident acceptance creates the existing post-collection payment balance. The extra weight does not consume another pickup or additional included monthly kilograms. Membership validity, pickup count and remaining included monthly allowance still apply. Existing accepted receipts are not rewritten. Specialist waste remains subject to its separate agreed collection terms.

Examples in standard hours, segregated waste: Basic 3 kg = ₹10 + ₹3; reward 2.5 kg = ₹1.50 extra; Gold 9 kg = ₹3 extra; Platinum 12 kg = ₹6 extra.
